# Umbrella Corp - Expense Reimbursement Policy (sample document)

## What can be claimed
- Meals while travelling: up to $40 per day.
- Taxis and ride-shares for business trips.
- Client entertainment, when pre-approved by a director.
- Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Approval rules
1. Any single expense over $250 needs written approval from your manager before you spend it.
2. Receipts are required for every expense over $25.
3. Expenses over $1,000 also need finance sign-off.

## Timing
- Submit your expense report within 30 days of the purchase, or it may be rejected.
- Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days.
