Umbrella Corp - Leave Policy (sample document)

1. Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 paid vacation days per year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their working hours. Leave accrues monthly, at 2 days per month.

2. Carry-over
Unused vacation days may be carried over into the next year for up to three months. After March 31, any remaining carried-over days expire.

3. Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

4. Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

5. How to request leave
Submit a leave request in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for vacations longer than 5 days. Your manager approves requests within 3 business days.
