# Umbrella Corp - New Employee Onboarding (sample document)

## Before day one
- HR sends your offer paperwork and a laptop shipping notice one week before you start.
- Your manager assigns an onboarding buddy who will answer day-to-day questions.

## Day one
1. Pick up your laptop and ID badge at the front desk by 9:30 AM.
2. Meet your manager and your buddy for a welcome coffee.
3. Complete the mandatory security awareness training before using any company system.
4. Set up your email, chat account and two-factor authentication with the IT helpdesk.

## First week
- Attend the company overview session on Tuesday at 10:00 AM.
- Review the team handbook and the code of conduct, and sign the acknowledgement form.
- Meet each member of your team for a 30-minute introduction.

## First 90 days
- Day 30: check-in with your manager on goals and expectations.
- Day 60: complete role-specific training modules.
- Day 90: probation review. Your manager and HR confirm your position.
