Umbrella Corp - IT Security Rules (sample document)

1. Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords must be changed every 180 days. Never reuse a password from another service. Use the company password manager to store credentials.

2. Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and all admin systems. Use the authenticator app. SMS codes are not permitted.

3. Devices
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may not be used to access customer data.

4. Reporting incidents
Report lost devices, suspicious emails or suspected data leaks to security@umbrella.example within 1 hour of noticing. Do not try to investigate on your own.

5. Data handling
Customer data must never be copied to personal cloud storage or sent through personal email. Confidential documents must be shared only with named colleagues, never with public links.
