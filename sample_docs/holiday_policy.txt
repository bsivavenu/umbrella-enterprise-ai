Umbrella Corp - Company Holidays Policy (sample document)

1. Public holidays
Umbrella Corp observes 10 public holidays per year. The office is closed on each of them.

2. Year-end closure
The office closes from December 24 to January 1. These days do not count against vacation allowance.

3. Floating holiday
Every employee receives 1 floating holiday per year, to use for a personal or religious occasion. It must be requested at least 1 week ahead.

4. Working on a public holiday
Employees who are required to work on a public holiday are paid 1.5 times their normal rate, or may take a day off in lieu within 60 days.
